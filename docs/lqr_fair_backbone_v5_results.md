# LQR fair_backbone_v5: completed results

All 15 final evaluations completed 500 episodes, 100,000 environment steps and 99,001 updates. Frozen configurations, 20 paired evaluation seeds per amplitude, finite returns and raw-return means were checked. Every arm has zero target shift and zero action anchor.

Cost is minus episode return; lower is better. Means and sample SDs below are over three independent training-seed means, not 60 independent trained models.

| Method | 1x Cost | 20x Cost | 50x Cost | 50x Seed SD |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| CS-BAPR | 17.64 | 30,086.07 | 1,444,055.06 | 198,142.99 |
| BAPR | 77.13 | 28,650.13 | 2,040,024.16 | 599,452.01 |
| RE-SAC | 39.87 | 87,989.20 | 2,212,955.06 | 423,214.31 |
| No SINDy | 67.59 | 24,961.44 | 1,411,837.85 | 111,229.32 |
| No NAU | 13.69 | 26,115.37 | 1,453,115.12 | 94,077.73 |

CS-BAPR reduces 50x mean cost by 29.21% versus BAPR and 34.75% versus RE-SAC, winning all three matched training seeds against both. The advantage is not uniform: at 20x its mean cost is 5.01% higher than BAPR's, despite winning two seeds.

The symbolic-alignment bundle improves ID and 2x/5x on all three seeds, but does not improve 20x/50x mean cost. At 50x, full CS-BAPR costs 2.28% more than no-SINDy, winning only two seeds. Replacing the arithmetic actor with the tanh MLP changes 50x mean cost by only 0.62% in CS-BAPR's favor; the paired-difference SD is 287,842 versus a mean gain of 9,060. These results do not establish either component's necessity for large-amplitude OOD. No-SINDy removes both symbolic critic alignment and actor Jacobian alignment; no-NAU is an architecture/regularization bundle, not a single-loss contrast.

At 50x, initial-state fitted-reference actions are 95% saturated (absolute component >0.95), versus 0% at 1x. Reference Jacobian Frobenius norm falls from about 2.016 to 0.128. This suggests that the stress test increasingly rewards saturated control rather than scale-sensitive action magnitudes; it is a diagnostic interpretation, not a demonstrated cause of the ablation result.

Known-model tanh-feedback cost is 1,234,718.75 at 50x, versus CS-BAPR's 1,444,055.06 and zero action's 6,365,310.48. These reused paired controls have the same frozen environment and evaluation seeds; they are non-learning diagnostics, not a constrained optimum.

## Limitations

Only three training seeds; no significance claim. All v5 workers used Torch 2.5.1+cu121, but GPU models differ. Baselines retain inherited stabilization and different preset regularization/behavior-cloning settings. The contemporaneous CS-BAPR runs replace, rather than augment, v4 runs with the same seeds. Fixed linear dynamics and initial-state extrapolation do not reproduce bus-style stochastic OOD. Historical results and manuscript tables remain unchanged.

## Next Step

Prioritize an environment-validity pilot before more seeds or loss-weight tuning. Introduce currently observable exogenous random loads/disturbances and stress their tail and occurrence frequency, rather than only enlarging the initial state. Check fixed-action-authority controllability and reference-policy saturation/cost first; choose severity using environment/control behavior, not CS-BAPR ranking. Keep LQR as a negative/control setting and freeze the same five-arm comparison before further formal training.
