# LQR critic_shift_v4: completed results

All 12 runs completed 500 episodes, 100,000 environment steps and 99,001 updates. Final-checkpoint returns use 20 paired evaluation seeds per amplitude; the table averages the three training-seed means. Higher return is better.

| Method | 1x | 2x | 5x | 10x | 20x | 50x |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| CS-BAPR | -1,118.16 | -18,386.24 | -407,560.19 | -1,260,100.76 | -2,918,633.05 | -8,765,828.10 |
| Action anchor | -14,960.69 | -42,770.08 | -123,495.55 | -528,161.37 | -1,537,670.18 | -5,536,347.57 |
| No target shift | -35.86 | -47.43 | -185.16 | -1,388.35 | -36,701.42 | -1,582,597.43 |
| Anchor + no target shift | -3.54 | -14.97 | -130.00 | -1,162.16 | -112,560.03 | -2,453,903.62 |

Removing the positive weight-norm bootstrap shift improves all three seeds at all six amplitudes, both with and without the action anchor. At 50x, mean cost falls 81.95% without the anchor and 55.68% with it. This shared critic-target term is the dominant failure identified by the factorial.

After removing the shift, anchoring improves every seed through 10x and gives near-reference ID return. At 20x/50x it wins only one seed out of three; average cost rises 206.69%/55.05%. At 50x, its smaller initial-state action mismatch (RMSE 0.677 versus 0.783) does not translate into better rollout return. Do not select the anchor as a uniformly superior OOD configuration.

The no-anchor/no-shift 50x cost is 1.583 million, versus 6.365 million for zero action and 1.235 million for known-model tanh feedback. These paired controls are post-hoc, non-learning diagnostics, not trained baselines or a constrained optimality certificate.

## Limitations

Only three independent training seeds; evaluation episodes are not extra training replicates. One no-anchor/no-shift run used local Torch 2.8.0+cu128, while the other runs used Torch 2.5.1+cu121. The fixed linear environment tests initial-state extrapolation, not bus-style stochastic dynamics. Historical BAPR/RE-SAC still used the positive shift, so their comparison cannot establish CS-BAPR component superiority.
