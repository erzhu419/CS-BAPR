# RandomLoad v6: frozen environment-validity pilot and comparison

Frozen before pilot or learning results. Keep the fixed double-integrator matrices, normalized actions [-1,1], force scale 5, costs and 200-step horizon from LQR. All resets use a radius-0.2 ball at every severity. Add action-independent external force to the physical transition; all five methods observe its current two components alongside the four physical state components, never its future values or severity label.

- Force refresh has probability 0.1 per step (mean dwell ten steps). A refresh is a storm with probability `0.05 * sqrt(severity)`. Background components are uniform within 0.1 times the force cap; storm magnitudes use `min(1, 0.1*(1+Pareto(1.5)))` with random signs. The cap is `0.05 * severity` per axis. At 50x it is 2.5, half the unchanged controlled-force bound of 5. Random draws do not branch on storm status, and actions do not alter the force stream.
- Non-learning pilot: severities 1/2/5/10/20/50, seeds 40000--40099, zero action, force cancellation only, physical feedback ignoring force, and physical feedback plus force compensation. Before dispatch require the compensated reference to beat zero at every severity, cost less than one fifth of zero at 50x, remain below 10% component saturation throughout, improve over force-blind feedback at 50x, and show at least fivefold storm occupancy versus ID. A failed pilot stops formal dispatch; no CS-BAPR ranking enters these choices.
- The reference is `tanh(-K*x-C*force)`. Fit physical matrices and the current-force coupling with controlled degree-1 SINDy on separate severity-1 trajectories; solve the four-state Riccati equation and least-squares force compensation. Stochastic future-force rows are excluded from feedback construction. The gain remains a 2x6 matrix, using existing reference/checkpoint serialization. No known-model coefficients enter a trained symbolic reference.
- After a passing pilot, five fresh arms use the v5 presets: CS-BAPR, BAPR, RE-SAC, no-SINDy, no-NAU, all with target shift 0 and action anchor 0. Only environment name and input dimension change. Seeds 0/1/2, 500 episodes each; 100,000 learner-interaction steps and 99,001 updates. Symbolic arms additionally collect 50 identification and five validation episodes (11,000 steps), then reseed training. Total training/identification collection is therefore 111,000 for symbolic arms versus 100,000 for others, excluding final evaluation; this is not a total-data-matched comparison. No coefficient search or efficiency change.
- Final checkpoint only; atomic full-state episode-boundary resume every 20 episodes. Evaluate each severity with paired seeds 10000--10019. Report returns and component-negative comparisons at all severities. OOD scales ongoing disturbance magnitude and storm frequency, not initial state, action authority, physical dynamics or reward weights.
- Frozen source and existing offline wheels; scheduler chooses idle compatible nodes/GPUs without preferences. Only small evaluation outputs return locally; checkpoints stay on workers. A six-episode, disjoint-seed-996 CUDA preflight validates startup, identification and final evaluation.

## Limitations

This is another controlled continuous-state diagnostic, not yet a standard Gym benchmark or a validation of general RL/bus-level performance. The disturbance is currently observed, unlike the hidden regimes in prior MuJoCo studies. The compensated tanh feedback is a non-learning control reference, not a constrained or stochastic optimum. Three training seeds do not establish significance. Old LQR negatives and manuscript tables remain unchanged.
